Pastimes & Passions

Hobbies & Lifestyle

Cross-Country Travel & Exploration

Even at 95 years old, staying in one place has never been her style. With family spread across numerous states, traveling nationwide allows her to stay actively engaged in the lives of her children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren.

Whether flying coast-to-coast or driving through scenic mountain routes, she treats every trip as a fresh adventure that keeps her energized and connected.

Family Genealogy & Photo Preservation

Preserving family history is a cornerstone of her daily routine. Having lived through nearly a century of changes, she curates extensive family photo albums and documents the lineages linking four living generations.

Her collection bridges early childhood memories growing up in Ocean Beach with modern family milestones, ensuring that family records are preserved for future generations.

Creating Arts and Crafts

A lifelong maker, Jolene directs much of her creative energy into handcrafted projects designed specifically for her grandchildren and great-grandchildren.

From personalized keepsakes to holiday decorations, crafting serves as a tangible way for her to share warmth, creativity, and meaningful gifts that family members treasure for years.
