About Jolene (Gma)

A Lifetime of Family, Community, and Adventure

Born on Central Avenue in San Diego on May 5, 1930 alongside her twin sister, Jolene spent her youth in Ocean Beach. As an adult, she dedicated her professional life to community education and early literacy, working in the children's library at Pepper Drive school to introduce young students to reading.

"Always be happy, don't be angry, if you can't do anything about it don't sweat it!"
— Jolene (Gma)
