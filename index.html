Celebrating 95 Years of Adventure & Devotion

Welcome to the living tribute to Jolene, affectionately known to family as Gma. Born on Cinco de Mayo in 1930 and raised in Ocean Beach, this site honors her rich life, her career in youth literacy, and her continued nationwide travels.

Milestones & Accomplishments

Generations of Family

Married to the love of her life and raising four healthy children, Jolene cherishes the close ties that unite four living generations across the country.

95-Year Milestone

Born on May 5, 1930 alongside her twin sister, Jolene's Cinco de Mayo birthday remains an annual focal point for large family get-togethers and celebration.

Cherished Celebrations

Whether attending beach gatherings or family reunions, she brings humor, energy, and joy to every milestone she shares with loved ones.

"Always be happy, don't be angry, if you can't do anything about it don't sweat it!"

— Jolene (Gma), on advice for younger generations
